What your child will learn

Speaking first

Every lesson is built around conversation. Your child practises real sentences out loud with games, role-play and stories, so confidence grows from the very first class.

Listening

Songs, stories and clear spoken English to train the ear.

Reading

From first words to short stories, at your child's level.

Writing

Sentences, then paragraphs, with gentle feedback.

Homework and assignments

Help with school English work so nothing gets left behind.

Children learn best when they feel relaxed and safe to try. In my lessons there is no pressure and no fear of mistakes, because mistakes are how we learn.

With an experienced teacher who knows how children learn, your child can enjoy English at their own pace.
